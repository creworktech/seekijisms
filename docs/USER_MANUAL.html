1. Welcome to Seekoji SMS

Seekoji SMS is an end-to-end Service Management System designed specifically for electrical equipment repair centers. Whether you manage single-item walk-in customers or bulk equipment intake from industrial clients, Seekoji SMS simplifies job tracking, diagnostics, budget approvals, repair workflows, and financial settlements.

Key Benefits for Your Workshop:
  • Pre-Generated Token Sequence: Know exact token numbers (e.g. #SES1, #SES2) before saving jobs.
  • Structured 8-Stage Pipeline: Complete visibility into every repair stage from intake to delivery.
  • Zero Duplication & Customer ID: Auto-generated Customer IDs (e.g. ID00001) linked to mobile numbers.
  • Fair Financial Dues: Clear distinction between active dues, paid orders, and rejected estimates with NO AMOUNT DUE (₹0) handling.

1.1 User Roles & Access Privileges

User Role Primary Responsibilities Accessible Modules & Action Scope
Admin Overall business oversight, financial settlements, staff creation, job & customer deletion. Access to ALL pages (Dashboard, JCC, All Jobs, Delivery, Customers, Accounts, Reports, Users, Settings). Only role allowed to delete records.
Intake Coordinator Customer registration, equipment intake, token receipt issuing, customer handover. Access to Job Intake Modal, Job Control Center, Customers Directory, and Delivery. Cannot alter financial settings or delete records.
Technician / Tester Equipment testing, technical fault reporting, repair work execution, final bill entry. Mainly operates inside Under Testing and Under Repair stages. Enters diagnostic findings and required repair budgets.

2. Interface Layout & Staff Account Setup

2.1 Navigation Bar & Main Views

The left sidebar provides instant 1-click access to all modules:

  • Dashboard: Executive summary tiles showing active job counts, revenue stats, and stage distribution charts.
  • Job Control Center (JCC): Interactive 8-stage Kanban workspace designed for daily operations.
  • All Jobs: Tabular view with advanced multi-filter search, sorting, and job drawer.
  • Delivery: Dispatch desk list showing items ready for customer collection.
  • Customers: Customer database with profile drawers, history logs, and delete options.
  • Accounts: Financial ledger tracking collected cash/UPI revenue and pending customer dues.
  • Reports: Exportable analytics reports (CSV and printable PDF work order receipts).
  • Users: Staff user account management (Admin only).
  • Settings: Configure token prefixes (SES), customer prefixes (C), and inspection fees.

2.2 Creating Staff User Accounts (Admin Only)

  1. Navigate to the Users menu in the sidebar.
  2. Click + Add New User.
  3. Fill in User Name, Email, Mobile Number, and Select Role (Admin, Intake Coordinator, or Technician).
  4. Enter the password in a single field (password confirmation field is removed for speed and simplicity).
  5. Click Save User. The staff member can now log in immediately.

3. Customer Registration & Lookup

Every customer profile is assigned a permanent system ID (e.g. ID00001). Seekoji SMS verifies mobile numbers in real time to prevent duplicate profiles.

3.1 Step-by-Step Customer Creation

  1. Click Customers in the sidebar and click + Add Customer (or click New Customer inside the Intake Modal).
  2. Type the customer's 10-digit mobile number. As you type, the system checks the database:
    • If mobile exists: System displays "Customer Already Registered" and auto-fills their details.
    • If mobile is new: System enables fields for Name, Email, Address, and City.
  3. Fill in Customer Name and click Save Customer.
  4. The new customer receives a permanent customer code (e.g. C00002).
Deleting Customer Records: Only Admins can delete customer accounts. Clicking the trash button triggers a custom confirmation modal to prevent accidental deletion.

4. Job Intake & Token Pre-Generation

The Job Intake Modal handles both single equipment intake and multi-product bulk intake.

4.1 Pre-Generated Token Sequence Feature

When you open the Intake Modal, Seekoji SMS automatically previews the exact token numbers assigned to each product (e.g., Token: #SES1, Token: #SES2) before you save to the database.

  1. Click the top right NEW JOBS button.
  2. Select or create the customer using the searchable customer dropdown.
  3. For Product 1: Enter Product Name (e.g. ACGT), Brand/Make (e.g. LG), Serial Number, Power Rating, and Customer Reported Fault. Notice the pre-assigned token badge Token: #SES1.
  4. If customer brought multiple units, click + Add Another Product. Notice Product 2 automatically gets badge Token: #SES2.
  5. Click Create Work Orders. All work orders are saved under consecutive token numbers in one click!
Token Sync Button: If another staff member created a job while your modal was open, click the Sync Tokens refresh icon to update your token preview instantly.

5. Job Control Center (JCC) - 8 Workflow Stages Guide

The Job Control Center (JCC) divides your service workflow into 8 clear sequential stages. Clicking any stage pill filters jobs instantly.

Stage Name What It Means Action Required & Next Steps
1. NEW JOBS Equipment newly logged at reception desk. Review reported fault, select a tester from dropdown, and click Assign Tester. (Or click Cancel Job if customer cancels at intake).
2. UNDER TESTING Tester is diagnosing hardware on testing bench. Tester opens job, inputs Technical Findings, and chooses:
• No Fault Found (Moves to Completed, inspection fee applied)
• Fault Confirmed (Enter Estimated Budget & move to Approval)
• Not Repairable (Moves to Completed, inspection fee applied)
3. AWAITING APPROVAL Estimated budget is presented to customer. Call customer with estimated budget.
• If Approved: Click Approve, enter approved budget, and assign repair technician. Moves to Under Repair.
• If Rejected: Click Estimate Rejected. Moves to Completed. UI displays NO AMOUNT DUE (₹0).
4. UNDER REPAIR Technician is actively repairing equipment. Technician replaces parts and finishes repair:
• Click Work Done: Enter final bill amount and work summary. Moves to Completed.
• Click Hold / Pending: If waiting for parts. Moves to Pending.
5. PENDING Work is temporarily paused (waiting for spare parts). Once spare parts arrive at workshop, click Resume Repair to return job to Under Repair.
6. COMPLETED / PAYMENT Repair complete or estimate rejected. Payment settlement stage. Review final bill:
• If Dues Payable: Select Payment Mode (Cash, UPI, Bank) and click Collect Payment & Release. Moves to Ready for Delivery.
• If Estimate Rejected / No Fault: System displays NO AMOUNT DUE (₹0). Click Release to Delivery.
7. READY FOR DELIVERY Equipment is packed and sitting at dispatch desk. When customer arrives for pickup, verify handover person and click Handover to Customer. Moves to Delivered.
8. DELIVERED Equipment successfully returned to customer. Archived completed job record. Contains full timeline audit history.

6. Financial Dues & Payment Status Explanation

Seekoji SMS clearly differentiates between orders requiring payment and zero-due orders:

  • PAID (₹X,XXX): Repair amount or inspection fee collected and settled via Cash, UPI, or Bank Transfer.
  • UNPAID (₹X,XXX): Final repair bill or inspection fee remains uncollected (released on credit or pending payment).
  • NO AMOUNT DUE (₹0): Automatically displayed when estimate was rejected by customer or no repair fee applies. Ensures staff do not confuse zero dues with unpaid bills!

7. Reports & Data Export

The Reports module allows workshop managers to inspect stage counts, total collected revenue, and export data:

  • CSV Export: Click Export CSV to download a spreadsheet of filtered work orders.
  • Print Summaries: Click Print Summary to print a clean physical paper work order receipt for shop archives.
